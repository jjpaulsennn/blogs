Florida heads to Columbia this weekend with more than another SEC win on the line.

The No. 8 Gators travel to face No. 25 Missouri on Saturday, October 3, with kickoff set for 3:30 p.m. ET on ABC. Florida enters the matchup 4-0 and coming off a statement 52-28 win over No. 4 Ole Miss, while Missouri provides a very different kind of test: a road game, a ranked opponent, and a recent history that Gator fans have not forgotten.

And apparently, neither has the team.

## The Photo Hanging Around Florida's Locker Room

This week's biggest storyline goes back to the last time Florida and Missouri met.

That game was not last season — the teams did not play in 2025 — but the 2023 meeting in Columbia still stings.

Florida had taken a 31-30 lead with 1:36 remaining and appeared to be closing in on a huge road upset of No. 9 Missouri. Then came one of the most painful plays of the season: facing fourth-and-17 from its own 33-yard line, Missouri quarterback Brady Cook found Luther Burden III for 27 yards and a first down.

A few plays later, Harrison Mevis kicked a 30-yard field goal with five seconds remaining to give Missouri a 33-31 win.

But the part that has followed Florida into 2026 is what came after.

Florida offensive lineman Bryce Lovett told reporters this week that a photo of former Gator defensive lineman Tyreak Sapp sitting on the ground after the loss, with Missouri players around him, has been posted throughout Florida's team areas.

That is not exactly subtle motivation.

For a Florida team trying to reestablish itself nationally under first-year head coach Jon Sumrall, this weekend is a chance to replace one of the program's most frustrating recent memories with a very different one.

## Florida vs. Missouri Has Been More Competitive Than You Might Think

Missouri only joined the SEC in 2012, but this matchup has already produced plenty of strange and memorable games.

Missouri currently leads the all-time series 7-6, including the 1966 Sugar Bowl meeting.

Since Missouri joined the SEC, neither team has been able to completely control the series. There have been blowouts in both directions, close finishes, overtime, and a few games that Gator fans would probably prefer not to revisit.

A few notable meetings:

- **2012 — Florida 14, Missouri 7:** Florida won the first SEC meeting between the two programs in Gainesville.
- **2014 — Missouri 42, Florida 13:** One of the strangest losses in recent Florida history. Missouri scored 42 points despite finishing with only 119 yards of offense.
- **2015 — Florida 21, Missouri 3:** The Gators returned the favor with a dominant defensive performance in Columbia.
- **2017 — Missouri 45, Florida 16:** Missouri handed Florida one of the series' most lopsided losses.
- **2018 — Missouri 38, Florida 17:** The Tigers came into Gainesville and beat a ranked Florida team by three touchdowns.
- **2020 — Florida 41, Missouri 17:** Florida controlled the matchup in The Swamp behind one of its strongest teams of the last decade.
- **2021 — Missouri 24, Florida 23:** Missouri won in overtime after successfully going for two.
- **2022 — Florida 24, Missouri 17:** The Gators bounced back with a home win.
- **2023 — Missouri 33, Florida 31:** The fourth-and-17 game, the last-second field goal, and the photo Florida is still looking at today.

The recent trend has favored Missouri in Columbia. Florida has lost its last two trips there, in 2021 and 2023.

## Why This Game Feels Different for Florida

Florida is not arriving in Columbia as a team trying to salvage a season.

The Gators are 4-0, ranked No. 8, and coming off a 52-point performance against Ole Miss. Under Sumrall, Florida has looked more physical, more confident, and more explosive than it has in recent seasons.

That makes Saturday an opportunity to prove the early-season success travels.

Winning at home is one thing. Winning a ranked SEC road game against a team that has caused Florida problems for more than a decade is another.

Missouri will also have the benefit of a Homecoming crowd at Memorial Stadium, making the atmosphere even tougher.

## The Revenge Factor Is Real — But Execution Still Matters

The locker-room photo gives this matchup an easy storyline, but Florida cannot win with motivation alone.

The 2023 loss is the perfect example.

Florida did enough late in that game to take the lead, but one defensive breakdown on fourth-and-17 changed everything. Missouri turned a nearly dead possession into a game-winning drive in seconds.

That is exactly the kind of detail Florida will need to handle better this time around.

Road SEC games usually come down to avoiding the plays that flip momentum: missed tackles, penalties, turnovers, special-teams mistakes, and failures to get off the field on third or fourth down.

If Florida can play with the same physicality it has shown through the first month of the season without allowing the emotions of the revenge storyline to take over, it will give itself a strong chance to change the series' recent direction.

## Final Thoughts

Florida does not need anyone to explain what happened the last time it went to Missouri.

The team has the picture.

Saturday is the chance to create a new one.

A ranked road game, a hostile Homecoming crowd, a one-game gap in the all-time series, and a painful finish still fresh enough to be taped to the walls of the facility — this matchup has everything Gator fans could want.

Now Florida has to finish the job.
