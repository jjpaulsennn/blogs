# Perch 2026 UF + Alabama SEO Architecture

## Goal

Build a search cluster around high-intent college football parking searches instead of relying on isolated blog posts.

## Internal-Linking Rules

1. Each school hub links to every matchup page.
2. Each matchup page links back to its school hub.
3. Every supporting blog post links to the most relevant matchup page and the school hub.
4. Use natural anchors such as:
   - UF football parking
   - parking near The Swamp
   - Alabama football parking
   - parking near Bryant-Denny Stadium
   - UF vs Oklahoma parking
   - Iron Bowl parking
5. Keep year-specific URLs live after each game. Do not recycle a 2026 URL into a future season.

## On-Page SEO Checklist

- Matchup + year in title.
- Stadium or city in title/description where natural.
- One clear H1.
- Helpful logistics before promotional copy.
- Stadium, date, city, and parking intent in first 100 words.
- FAQs that answer booking timing, walking distance, arrival, and reservation questions.
- Strong CTA near the top and again near the bottom.
- SportsEvent JSON-LD on production matchup landing pages.
- Submit each new production URL in Google Search Console.
- Add links from the school-level parking page immediately after publishing.

## Conversion Notes

Perch copy should emphasize:
- reserved parking before leaving home
- digital payment and confirmation
- private spaces near the venue
- avoiding last-minute circling and cash
- host inventory that can include driveways, yards, private lots, schools, churches, and businesses

Do not make exact walking-distance, availability, or sellout claims unless supported by live inventory.
