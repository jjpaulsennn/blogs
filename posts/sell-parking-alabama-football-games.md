## What Makes a Great Perch Host Listing?

The best listings remove uncertainty for the customer.

### Show Exactly Where People Will Park

Use clear photos so drivers understand what they are booking.

If you are offering a driveway, yard, or side lot, make it obvious where cars should go.

### Explain the Walk

If you are close to Bryant-Denny Stadium, campus, downtown, or another event venue, tell customers what the walk is like.

A simple description such as "10-minute walk to Bryant-Denny" can make a listing much easier to understand.

### Give Good Arrival Instructions

Tell guests which entrance to use, where to pull in, and how you want cars arranged.

Good instructions can prevent confusion when dozens of surrounding streets are packed.

### Mention Any Extras

Depending on your property, customers may value features such as:

- Tailgating space
- Shade
- Lighting
- Easy exit access
- Room for trucks or SUVs
- Restroom access, if you intentionally offer it
- A host available on-site

Only advertise amenities you actually plan to provide.
