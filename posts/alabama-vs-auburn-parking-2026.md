## Why Iron Bowl Parking Requires a Plan

Big rivalry games attract more than the usual home crowd.

Friends and family travel in. Alumni return. Tailgates get bigger. People who might skip another game make sure they are in town for this one.

That puts extra pressure on convenient parking around campus.

Earlier than you would for an average game.

## What Matters Most in an Iron Bowl Parking Spot?

Think beyond pure distance.

Compare:

- walk to the stadium
- ease of entry
- postgame exit route
- tailgating rules
- space for trucks or SUVs
- whether your group can park together
- arrival instructions

A slightly farther spot with an easier exit may be better for one fan, while a closer tailgating location may be worth more to another.
